Office Asset Loan

Manage Office Inventory & Borrowing Lifecycles in Odoo 19 CE

Office Asset Loan is a lightweight yet robust Odoo 19 custom module tailored for internal company use. It seamlessly records shared office equipment (projectors, spare laptops, cameras, cables) and tracks every loan request with complete transparency.

View on GitHub

Key Features & Business Rules

Asset Inventory (`office.asset`)

  • Unique Asset Codes: Enforced at database level via models.Constraint (BR-1).
  • Real-time Availability: Dynamic computed status toggling between free and borrowed.
  • Direct Smart Button: Quick one-click access to all historical loans for each asset.
  • Condition Tracking: Good, Fair, and Damaged states.

Loan Management (`office.asset.loan`)

  • Auto Sequence Numbering: Generates formatted codes like LOAN/0001 (BR-3).
  • Schedule Constraints: Due date cannot precede loan date (BR-2).
  • Borrow Safeguards: Blocks double loans and checkout of damaged gear (BR-4).
  • One-Click Return: Auto-records return timestamp and frees the asset (BR-5).
  • Integrity Protection: Prevents deletion of active/returned loans (BR-7).
  • Overdue Detection: Automated warning badge & visual indicators.
